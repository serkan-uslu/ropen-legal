Privacy Policy

Effective October 11, 2026 · ROPEN for iOS (com.lunivo.ropen)

ROPEN is developed by Serkan Uslu ("we"). This policy explains what data the app handles. In short: we run no servers, we have no user accounts, and your game progress never leaves your iPhone. Data is collected only by the advertising and purchase services described below.

Data stored on your device

Game progress, stars, statistics, streaks, badges, hint balance and settings are stored locally on your iPhone. We cannot see them. Remove them anytime with Settings → Reset All Data or by deleting the app.

Tracking and personalized ads

On first launch iOS asks whether ROPEN may track you across apps and websites. If you allow it, AdMob may use your Advertising Identifier to show personalized ads and measure them. If you do not allow it, no Advertising Identifier is shared and you receive non-personalized ads. You can change this anytime in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Where required (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland), Google's consent form is shown before any ads are requested.

Buying Remove Ads stops all ads, and no further ad requests are made.

What we do not do

We do not sell personal data, we do not build profiles, and we do not collect your name, email, contacts, photos or precise location.

Children

ROPEN is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal data. Contact us if you believe a child has provided data through the services above.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data on our side, requests about ad or purchase data are best directed to Google or RevenueCat. We will gladly help: info@lunivo.games.

Changes

We will update this page when the app's data practices change, and adjust the effective date above.
