Support

Questions, bugs or ideas? Write to info@lunivo.games. Please include your iPhone model and iOS version.

I bought Remove Ads but still see ads

Open Settings → Restore Purchases while signed in with the same Apple ID you used to buy. Purchases are tied to your Apple ID, not to the device.

My progress is gone

Progress is stored only on your iPhone. Deleting the app or using Settings → Reset All Data removes it, and it cannot be recovered.

How do I play?

Drag the knots so that no two ropes cross. Each mode adds one rule (frozen knots, a move budget, a clock, hidden crossings and more); the first level of every mode explains it.

Ads and tracking

ROPEN is free and shows occasional ads, never in the middle of a puzzle. If you allowed tracking and change your mind, turn it off in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Details: Privacy Policy.
